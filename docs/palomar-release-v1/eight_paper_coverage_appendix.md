The counts are paper **rows**, not unique results. The paper ledger has **674** row objects: 506 `exact`, 140 `exact_or_stronger`, 25 `modulo_named_input`, and 3 `none`. Its Comparator fields read 612 `compared`, 34 `pending`, and 28 `not_applicable`. The ledger's stored Lean summary says 24 `modulo_named_input` and 4 `none`; those two counts disagree with its own row objects, which this appendix uses for per-row dispositions. The full isolated Comparator corpus separately contains **132 configurations** with **1,765 distinct selected declaration names**. The eight proposed release configurations are a small allowlist within that corpus; neither 674 nor 1,765 is a submission target, novelty count, or Palomar registration count.

The machine-readable file gives each of the 674 rows its source line, Lean/Comparator ledger status, historical comparison entries, editorial disposition and mapping basis. For the four newly mapped problems, selected rows carry a full source declaration name found both in the selected Solution proof and the coverage row. The #1049 short-paper 31/4 corollary uses a separately recorded statement crosswalk because its older source theorem has a different name. `checked_unbound_outside_registration` means the row has historical compared support but no exact selected-source mapping was established for the entire paper row. `selected_claim_with_pending_paper_row` keeps a selected source declaration separate from the ledger's pending paper-row comparison. `selected_conditional_with_named_inputs` preserves its hypotheses. `legacy_weaker_endpoint_only` applies to both #1041 Hausdorff paper rows: the baseline selection contains the older variation statement, not those stronger endpoints.
